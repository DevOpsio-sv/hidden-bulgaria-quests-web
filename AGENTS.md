## 1. What this is

Not a website about a country. **The first checkpoint of a quest that continues in the real world.** Everything on screen is inside the fiction.

The product answers one question, asked in the first ten seconds and never fully answered: *Bulgaria is the only country in Europe whose name has never changed — 681 to now. How?*
The answer: **people carried it.**

---

## 2. Before any task — three things

1. **Name your Epic and Story.** Work only inside it. Never touch a surface belonging to another.
2. **Name your layer.** Every surface is either:
   - **TOLD LAYER** — made to make someone *feel* something. Voice + Inscription faces, HELD/WIDE/CLOSE air, photography law, motion law.
   - **USEFUL LAYER** — made to let someone *do* something. Utility face, SET air, conventional controls, clarity over atmosphere. All commerce lives here.
   **If you cannot say which layer, stop and ask.** ~15 rules resolve differently depending on the answer.
3. **Cite your Principle** (P1–P16) and the rule IDs in scope. Put them in the PR description with what you removed.

---

## 3. The ten stop conditions

**Stop and ask. Do not guess, do not compromise, do not proceed "temporarily."**

1. The task requires violating the Bible.
2. You cannot name the layer.
3. The task needs a component that doesn't exist.
4. The task needs a colour, spacing value, typeface, radius or shadow that isn't defined.
5. The task needs copy in a language you weren't given.
6. The task needs an asset that isn't in the repo.
7. The task asserts something about history.
8. The task would add a second forward path to a narrative surface.
9. The task would make the world look complete.
10. You're about to write `TODO`, a placeholder, or "coming soon."

## 6. The twelve absolutes

1. Never ship a placeholder, draft label, TODO, or "coming soon."
2. Never reference an asset that isn't in the deploy.
3. Never use stock, illustrated, or **AI-generated** imagery. Ever.
4. Never autoplay sound.
5. Never time-gate content. Duration governs transitions, never disclosure.
6. Never add motion that is neither **weather** (light, dust, water, breath, wind) nor **consequence** (the visitor caused it).
7. Never render a hover affordance on something that isn't interactive.
8. Never present more than one forward path on a narrative surface.
9. Never remove a focus indicator; never derive text colour from `opacity`; never block the primary scroll axis.
10. Never hardcode a user-visible string — all copy comes from the locale files.
11. Never assert anything about history without a source and a named reviewer.
12. Never make the world look complete or completable.

## 7. The three verbs

The entire product is operated with three: **Descend** (scroll) · **Look through** (Guardian Sight) · **Pass through** (enter a threshold). There is no fourth. If your task needs one, it's the wrong task.

## 9. Judgement, when the rules run out

- When in doubt, **remove**.
- When two solutions are equal, choose the **quieter** one.
- When something is technically correct and emotionally wrong, **it is wrong**.
- When a convention from another site suggests itself, that is a reason for **suspicion**, not adoption.
- **Never optimise a detail before the experience it sits inside is right.**
- Where this document is silent, **ask**. Silence is not permission.
