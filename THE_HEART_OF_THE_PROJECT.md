# THE HEART OF THE PROJECT

**Unlocking Bulgaria**

*This is the highest authority in this repository. The Product Bible tells you what to build. The governance rules tell you what not to break. This tells you why any of it is worth doing. When a decision is hard, come back here. When this document and any other document disagree, this one wins.*

---

## I. THE ONE THING THAT SURVIVED

Months of design produced hundreds of ideas. Almost all of them were good. Almost all of them were wrong in exactly the same way — every single one ended with a person still holding their phone.

One realization survived:

> **People do not want another app about Bulgaria.
> They want to find out that Проходна exists — and then go stand inside it.**

The app is not the destination. The app is the reason someone puts on their shoes.

If the product is working, its most important moment happens when the screen goes dark and the car starts.

---

## II. WHY THIS DESERVES TO EXIST

Nine million people come to Bulgaria every year. Most of them leave knowing three things: there is a valley of roses, Plovdiv is old, the sea is warm.

Meanwhile:

In Магура there are paintings on the wall that people made between eight and four thousand years before Christ, and almost nobody who visits this country will ever hear the name.

At Дуранкулак the dead have been lying facing east for six and a half thousand years — older than the pyramids, older than writing, four thousand years older than the word *Bulgaria*.

At Варна there is gold that was worked by human hands before anyone anywhere else on earth worked gold.

And in a limestone cave near Карлуково, the ceiling has two openings in it, side by side, shaped like eyes, and people have been standing underneath them and looking up for longer than we have records for.

None of this is hidden. All of it is unvisited.

That gap — between what is here and what anyone knows is here — is not a marketing problem. It is a loss happening quietly, every year, to everyone on both sides of it. We exist to close it, one person and one place at a time.

---

## III. WHY BULGARIA NEEDS THIS SPECIFICALLY

In 681 a name entered history. In 1018 the state that carried it was destroyed and did not exist for one hundred and sixty-seven years. In 1185 it came back — the only medieval Slavic state ever to reconstitute itself after total conquest — and it came back with the same name.

The state died. The name did not.

That is not a fun fact. That is the single strangest and most beautiful thing about this country, and it raises a question nobody has ever properly answered:

> **Как България е опазила Името си 1345 години?**
> **How has Bulgaria kept its Name for 1345 years?**

We are not building content around that question. The question is the product. Every place we send someone to is a partial answer, and the full answer is not written anywhere — it can only be assembled by a person who physically went to enough places to feel it.

There is no other country where this exact product is possible, because there is no other country with this exact wound and this exact survival.

---

## IV. THE MEMORY WE ARE ACTUALLY MAKING

We are not in the information business. Information is available. Wikipedia has been free for twenty-five years and it has moved almost nobody toward Белоградчик.

We are in the business of the thing that happens ninety seconds after someone arrives somewhere, when the wind is wrong and their feet hurt and they are looking at a field, and a voice tells them what happened on that ground — and they look up.

That is what we make. Not knowledge. **The moment knowledge lands in a body that is standing in the right place.**

The memory we are building is never "I used a nice app." It is:

*"We drove three hours on a Saturday. My daughter found the marker before I did. We stood under the eyes."*

Ten years later the app will not be in that memory at all. Good. That is what winning looks like.

---

## V. WHAT HAPPENS INSIDE THE PERSON

Three states, in order. This is the entire arc of the product.

**Visitor.** Arrives to look at something. The place is an object. They are outside it.

**Witness.** Something reverses. The place stops being looked at and starts doing the looking. In Проходна the Keeper's line is exact and we should never improve on it: *"I was not only seeing. I was seen."*

**Пазител — Keeper.** Now they carry something. Not a badge. An obligation. They know a thing about this land that most people who live here do not know, and knowing it makes them responsible for passing it on.

Nobody is a Keeper because they finished a tutorial. They are a Keeper because they physically went to sixty-four places across a whole country, some of them over several years, and the land is different for them now.

You cannot buy this transformation. You can only walk it.

---

## VI. THE EMOTIONAL LOOP WE PROTECT ABOVE ALL

Everything in this ecosystem — every screen, every seal, every price, every line of narration — exists to keep this loop turning:

> *"I never knew Bulgaria had places like this."*
> ↓
> *"I want to go."*
> ↓
> *"I'm going this weekend."*
> ↓
> *"I want another place."*

Test every decision against the loop. Not against engagement. Not against session length. Not against anything a dashboard can show you.

**A feature that increases time in the app and decreases time on the road is a failed feature, no matter what the numbers say.**

We are the only product we know of that is trying to make its own screen time go down.

---

## VII. WHAT WE BELIEVE

**The place is the product.** The app is a door. Doors are not admired; they are walked through.

**Bulgarian is the source, not a translation.** Every word is written in Bulgarian first and every other language carries the *meaning* forward, never the English phrasing. *Пазителят* must arrive in German as **Hüter** — with all its weight — and never as a thinner word borrowed through English. Fourteen languages, one origin.

**Truth first, then symbol.** We say what is known. Where we interpret, the interpretation is clearly a poem, not a fact. We will never invent a history for a country whose entire story is that it refused to be invented by anyone else.

**Everyone gets in.** Subtitles always on. Narration always spoken. Guardian Sight always optional, with a complete path that never needs a camera. Text readable at twenty-six points in direct sun, because the person reading it is sixty-two years old and standing in a field. Accessibility here is not compliance. It is the difference between a national memory and a private club.

**Pay once. Keep forever.** No rentals. No expiry. No relocking anything a person already earned by being somewhere. What you collected is yours the way a photograph is yours.

**Never sell into a sacred moment.** No purchase prompt has ever improved a revelation. Story first, price after, always, without exception.

---

## VIII. THE PHILOSOPHY

The Bulgarian word for the two epochs is the whole philosophy: **Преди Името** and **След Името** — Before the Name and After the Name.

Before the Name does not mean emptiness. It means the land was already full. Already remembering. The Thracian kings under their mounds never heard the word *Bulgaria*, and they are part of this story anyway, because the story is not about a state — it is about a piece of ground that has been holding onto things for a very long time.

This is why Проходна is the first place a Keeper ever stands. It teaches the rule that governs everything after it:

> **Земята вече помнеше.**
> The land was already remembering.

Bulgaria did not begin when it was named. It inherited a country that had been paying attention for millennia. Our job is to introduce one person at a time to that fact, in the only way it can honestly be delivered — on site, in weather, with their own eyes.

And at the very end, after four Master Keys assembled from four corners of a real country, the answer arrives in four lines that must never be paraphrased, softened, explained, or previewed:

> Черният Камък помни.
> Живата Искра събужда.
> Златното Копие защитава.
> Белият Пламък освещава Земята, Душата и Тялото.

Nobody earns those four lines cheaply. That is the point of them.

## IX. THE EXPERIENCE WE PROTECT

**The arrival.** The seconds between GPS confirming and the first word of narration. Nothing is ever allowed to occupy that space — no ad, no upsell, no rating request, no tip, no badge.

**The mystery.** We do not answer the central question early. Not in the App Store description, not in the intro, not by accident in a checkpoint written by someone in a hurry. A thousand-year question deserves to stay open.

**The silence.** Проходна opens with roughly thirty words and a held breath. The instinct to fill quiet with content is the single most dangerous instinct on this team.

**The scale.** Four Domains. Sixteen Routes. Sixty-four real places. For many Keepers this will take years and several trips. We will never shrink it to fit a weekend. The size *is* the meaning.

**The final formula.** Exact. Forever. In Bulgarian.

---

## X. WHAT WE NEVER BECOME

Not a map. Maps show you where things are; we tell you why you should be there.

Not an encyclopedia. Facts that do not move feet are not our business.

Not a game. No points, no streaks, no leaderboards, no daily login rewards, no anxiety engineered to bring someone back to a screen. Our relics are memory objects tied to a real place a person really went. The moment one of them becomes a score, we have lost.

Not a tourism brochure. We will never call a place "picturesque," "charming," "hidden gem," or "must-see." Калиакра does not need adjectives. It needs two thousand six hundred years of context and then a person standing on it.

Not fake mysticism. We are working with a real Orthodox tradition, real Thracian sanctuaries, real Muslim and Roma communities in the Родопите, real graves. Nothing here is decorated. Everything here is respected or left alone.

Not an AR company. Guardian Sight is beautiful and it is optional and it will never be the reason anyone buys anything. If we ever find ourselves designing a place around the AR instead of designing the AR around the place, stop and reread this page.

Not a subscription trap. Ever.

## XI. WHAT SUCCESS ACTUALLY MEANS

Not downloads. Not MAU. Not rating.

Success is a line of cars on the road to Карлуково on a Saturday morning in April that was not there last year.

Success is a village of two hundred people with a coffee stand that suddenly sells out by noon.

Success is a Bulgarian, born here, forty years old, standing in Магура saying out loud: *I did not know this was in my country.*

Success is a woman in Berlin who has now been to Bulgaria four times, is missing eleven checkpoints, and has already booked the fifth trip.

Success is a seventy-year-old finishing a route because the subtitles were big enough and nothing required a camera.

Success is a family arguing in the car about which Domain to do next.

Success is one Keeper, years in, reading four lines of Bulgarian on a screen and going quiet — because they have personally stood in every place those lines were assembled from, and now they know the answer in their body instead of in their head.

And the truest measure, the one no analytics tool will ever report:

**Somewhere, a place that was going to be forgotten is not going to be forgotten, because enough strangers kept showing up.**

> # If it does not end with someone standing there, we failed.

Everything else is detail.
